Privacy Policy

Last updated: November 18, 2026

Marquee (“we”) is a personal film and TV watch diary. This policy explains what we collect and how we use it.

Information we collect

  • Account details you provide (email, name, password or Google sign-in identifiers).
  • Diary data you create (watched titles, episode progress, ratings, favorites, watchlists, imports).
  • Technical data needed to run the service (authentication tokens, device tokens if you enable notifications).

How we use information

We use your data only to operate Marquee: sign you in, sync your diary across devices, show stats, and improve reliability. We do not sell your personal data.

Third parties

Show and movie metadata and images come from TMDB. Sign-in may use Google. Hosting and email providers process data as needed to run the service.

Retention & deletion

You can delete your account in the Marquee app (Settings → Delete account). That removes your account and associated diary data from our servers.
